# Expression Challenge

The T7.2 game shuffles the seven class indices and builds a round ledger. Each round gives three seconds to prepare and eight seconds to play. A correct observed label needs at least three consecutive qualifying frames spanning 500 ms, with no adjacent matching-frame gap over 1,500 ms. The raw observation must be fresh and distinct, with a top-two lead of at least 0.04 under the challenge classifier. A stable match earns 100 points; seven matches total 700.

The browser [game reducer](../frontend/src/challenge/rounds.js) applies post-settlement rules and total points over [base game state](../frontend/src/challenge/rules.js). [`challengeSignal.js`](../frontend/src/challenge/signal.js) translates raw LIVE observations into game statuses. [`useChallengeT72.js`](../frontend/src/challenge/useChallenge.js) connects the clock, browser lifecycle and camera session. A separate target/detected display lets the player distinguish instruction from observation. Failure modes include timeout, no face, ambiguity, stale frame and service failure. Pause/retry/reopen, early finish and camera release have explicit test coverage.

T7.2 full controlled evidence recorded seven rounds and 200/700. A later controlled endurance check recorded 11 games across 631.59 seconds. Those conditions describe test coverage. Game events have no training or validation role. See [test details](TESTING_AND_VALIDATION.md).
